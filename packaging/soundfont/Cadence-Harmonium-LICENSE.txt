Cadence Harmonium.sf2 — sources, licenses and attribution
==========================================================

"Cadence Harmonium.sf2" is a SoundFont built by Cadence from real
harmonium recordings. It contains 38 single notes (C2 to D5).

Original recording
------------------
"Harmonium Samples - All Keys and Drones" by Donya Quick, recorded in the
Euterpea Studio at Yale University's Department of Computer Science.
https://freesound.org/people/donyaquick/sounds/330410/
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Edited samples (the files this SoundFont is built from)
-------------------------------------------------------
"harmonium" samples from tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky,
edited from the recording above (trimmed, ramped, volume-matched,
normalized, noise-reduced).
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
(commit 622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5, samples/harmonium/*.wav)
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
("Samples: CC-by 3.0", tonejs-instruments README)

Changes made by Cadence
-----------------------
Cadence converted these samples into a SoundFont 2 file
(scripts/build-harmonium-sf2.py in the Cadence source): each note is
trimmed to about 1-2.5 seconds, tuned to A440 with a per-note pitch
correction, given a crossfaded sustain loop, and mapped across the
keyboard with a short attack and a 0.2 s release. No other material is
added.
