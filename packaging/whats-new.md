New in 1.4.0
- Major UI rework: a warm, hardware-style look with new fonts (Barlow and IBM Plex Mono), tape-orange accents, LCD readouts and a cleaner instrument list
- Redesigned mixer: flat channel modules with clearer meters and values
- New instrument: a real sampled harmonium (search "harmonium" in the library, or pick it from quick add)
- Notes you play now sustain while held: computer keyboard, the Keyboard tab and MIDI keyboards, sustain pedal included
- The update screen now lists what's new in each version
